A LIFE IN ONE FRUIT

MANGIFERA INDICA · A SCROLL IN FOUR SEASONS

MANGO

One fruit, lived all the way through. Scroll to ripen it.

01 WHOLE / RIPE

FIRST — THE SKIN

Sun-fed.

Heavy with afternoon. A skin like warm lacquer — amber bleeding into a blush of red — holding a whole season of light.

02 OPEN / FLESH

THEN — THE GOLD

It gives.

A blade, a breath — and it opens. Fiber and nectar, coral going to gold. The inside of summer, still warm.

03 SEED / STONE

BENEATH — THE STONE

The core.

Past the sweetness, a stone. Fibrous, pale, patient — carrying the entire idea of a tree folded inside.

04 SPROUT / GREEN

AND THEN — AGAIN

Again.

The stone splits. A green thought reaches for the light. The fruit was never the end — only the envelope a beginning travelled in.

Every ending, folded quietly
into a beginning.
