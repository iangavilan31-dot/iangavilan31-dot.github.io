      What it is measuring

      Three things, kept apart on purpose. Notes is whether the right pitches arrived, in order. Intonation is how far, in cents, the ones you played sat from where they were written. Timing reports two numbers, because sitting consistently behind the click and scattering either side of it are different habits with different fixes — only the scatter can fail the gate.

      Pitch is found with YIN over a 2048-sample window; onsets come from spectral flux and are then validated against the pitch track, so a note release is not mistaken for a note. Measured on synthesised material with a known answer, the detector is within 0.7 cents across the viola's whole range. On a real instrument in a real room it will be worse, and this instrument will still only ever report what it actually measured.
