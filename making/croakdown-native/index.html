a page from the making of / Native prototype

CRACKDOWN

An isometric frog co-op roguelite with an evolving arena, enemies, and character motion.

the curiositythe buildthe practice

01 / the question behind it

Can two little frog fighters make a shared arena feel weighty and cooperative?

CRACKDOWN rebuilds the earlier frog-combat idea as an isometric Godot arena. Five waves combine directional sword sweeps, tongue setups, independent local players, upgrades, revival and a final champion.

02 / from idea to something playable

how the pieces came together.

  1. 01

    Keep the arena open for the fight

    Placed ruins, reeds, water, reflections and foreground sorting create depth without filling the middle with obstacles. There is no single static background image.

  2. 02

    Give each frog its own action

    Independent movement and attack controls support two local players. Directional sword sweeps, tongue setups and a downed-partner revive connect their actions.

  3. 03

    Rig the artwork and finish the run

    Eight painterly source views drive deforming meshes with planted feet, torso lean and separate equipment. Five waves and upgrade choices lead to a final champion.

03 / the skills in the work

what this helped me practice.

The work develops local input separation, combat progression and rigged 2D animation. Painterly source views are deformed with a textured mesh, while attached equipment and planted foot motion give the frogs a more physical presence.

  • Godot
  • Local co-op
  • 2D mesh animation
  • Combat systems
  • Native export

a note on the process.

The rebuild makes animation and shared input part of the core design. A fighter needs to appear planted before its weapon can feel heavy.

where the project stands

Playable native arena prototype; authored enemy and attack animation still needs polish.
