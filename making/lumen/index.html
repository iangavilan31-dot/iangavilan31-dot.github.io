Lumen

Guide the last light through a growing swarm. Collect embers and choose the next upgrade.

the curiositythe buildthe practice

01 / the question behind it

Can the last small light stay readable while the darkness around it grows?

Lumen is a Canvas survival game with a luminous player, growing enemy pressure and collectible embers. Experience leads to upgrade choices, while a procedural audio layer and a restrained light palette support the survival loop.

02 / from idea to something playable

how the pieces came together.

  1. 01

    Build the light-and-swarm contrast

    The player and ember pickups stand apart from the shades. Enemy spawning increases pressure while the drawing system keeps the player’s position visible.

  2. 02

    Make survival change the next decision

    Embers feed experience and open upgrade choices. The world freezes during level-up so a player can read and choose without losing the run in the background.

  3. 03

    Complete the restart loop

    Menu, playing, pause, level-up and defeat have explicit states. A best score is stored between visits, giving the short run a persistent target.

03 / the skills in the work

what this helped me practice.

The project develops 2D game-loop design, progression choices and visual hierarchy under pressure. It also uses browser storage for a best-run record and explicit menu, pause, level-up and defeat states.

  • Canvas 2D
  • JavaScript
  • Survival progression
  • Visual hierarchy
  • Procedural audio

a note on the process.

A survival game can get visually busy quickly. Holding the player, danger and reward in separate value ranges keeps the important decisions visible.
