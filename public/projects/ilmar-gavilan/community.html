Teaching, outreach, and cultural impact through music

Faculty and Masterclass Educator

Public biographies identify Gavilán as violin faculty at Manhattan School of Music, a performer-teacher affiliated with Montclair State University, and an artist who gives international masterclasses and coaching.

Harlem Quartet Mission and Residency Work

Harlem Quartet was founded through the Sphinx Organization with a mission to diversify classical music and reach young audiences. Their residencies and school outreach remain a major part of that public-facing work.

Family, Heritage, and Cross-Cultural Exchange

Through collaborations with his brother Aldo López-Gavilán and the story documented in Los Hermanos/The Brothers, Gavilán's public work has also become a cultural bridge linking Cuban musical heritage, family history, and contemporary performance.
