COMMON QUESTIONS

How is the Digital SAT different from the old SAT?

The format, passage types, and math structure all changed. The Digital SAT uses adaptive modules — your Module 2 difficulty is determined by Module 1 performance. Most tutors are still teaching the old test.

How many sessions before I see improvement?

Most students see meaningful score movement within 5–8 sessions. Realistic goals depend on baseline score and availability — we discuss this in your free score analysis.

Do you guarantee a score increase?

We don't guarantee specific scores — no honest tutor does. We do guarantee expert preparation, a clear strategy, and the kind of practice that produces real improvement.
