The redesign, step by step

You don’t just get a new site.
You watch it take shape.

Scroll through the five stages every project moves through — from the site you have today to the one that finally does it justice.

Concept — a fictional café

  1. 01Current

    We start with what's live today — and read it the way a first-time visitor does.

  2. 02Analysis

    Every friction point named: unclear next step, slow hero, buried hours, hard on mobile.

  3. 03Wireframe

    Structure before decoration. We settle hierarchy and flow in plain grey boxes.

  4. 04System

    Type scale, colour, and spacing become a system — consistent on every page, not a theme.

  5. 05Final

    The finished site: calm, fast, and pointed at one clear action.
