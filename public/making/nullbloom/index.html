a page from the making of / Native prototype

Nullbloom

A night-shift reclamation game in a drowned industrial city, with a hostile double that learns from the player.

the curiositythe buildthe practice

01 / the question behind it

How can a familiar industrial place become unsettling when something in it learns from the player?

Nullbloom is a Godot night-shift reclamation prototype set in a drowned industrial city. Its current portfolio slice centers on a hostile double that learns from the player.

02 / from idea to something playable

how the pieces came together.

  1. 01

    Establish an ordinary task in an uneasy place

    The night-shift reclamation setting gives the player work to do inside the drowned city. That everyday task is the frame for the threat.

  2. 02

    Give the threat a recognizable relationship

    The hostile double connects the danger to the player’s own behavior. The design question is how clearly that relationship can be communicated during play.

03 / the skills in the work

what this helped me practice.

The prototype explores environmental atmosphere, encounter design and the readability of a pursuing threat. Its current presentation is a focused concept and development slice rather than a complete released campaign.

  • Godot
  • Atmospheric world design
  • Encounter prototyping
  • Threat readability

a note on the process.

The core design question is how a player notices that the double is responding to them. Readable cause and effect matters as much as the atmosphere around the encounter.

where the project stands

Native development prototype; source and a public playable build are not currently linked.
