Cloudline Chaos

A wingsuit extraction prototype with a bot crew and midair rescue mechanics.

the curiositythe buildthe practice

01 / the question behind it

Can a risky wingsuit descent become a shared rescue story?

Cloudline Chaos is a Godot wingsuit-extraction prototype with a bot crew and midair rescue mechanics. Its focus is the relationship between flight, danger and getting the crew out.

02 / from idea to something playable

how the pieces came together.

  1. 01

    Start with the flight situation

    The wingsuit gives traversal a clear identity: forward motion, altitude and a landing opportunity are part of the same problem.

  2. 02

    Put the crew inside that problem

    A bot crew and midair rescue mechanics connect movement to another character’s situation, making extraction more than reaching a destination alone.

  3. 03

    Give the descent a destination

    Extraction ties the flight and rescue ideas together. The plane in the current prototype image establishes a point of departure, while the wingsuit and rescue premise give that departure a purpose.

03 / the skills in the work

what this helped me practice.

The prototype explores 3D movement, spatial readability and the design of cooperative rescue situations. Those are the most useful skills to foreground while the project is still a focused development slice.

  • Godot
  • Flight prototyping
  • 3D spatial design
  • Co-op interaction design

a note on the process.

The strongest design question is whether a player can read a rescue opportunity while still controlling their own flight. Keeping the crew’s situation visible is central to the idea.

where the project stands

PC-made wingsuit extraction prototype. The current portfolio shows a gameplay still; a public playable build is not linked.
