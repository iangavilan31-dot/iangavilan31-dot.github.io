INK/OUT

A comic-book rooftop heist in three dimensions, with a city to cross and a job to pull off.

the curiositythe buildthe practice

01 / the question behind it

Could taking color back from a city become a short interactive heist?

INK/OUT builds a procedural Three.js rooftop district around a living-ink character and a sequence of color-taking missions. Camera choreography, moving geometry and a print-style shader turn each action into a brief visual scene.

02 / from idea to something playable

how the pieces came together.

  1. 01

    Construct the district

    Buildings, roof machinery, bridges, signs and an elevated train create the city from geometry. Static geometry is merged by material while recolorable facades remain independent.

  2. 02

    Animate the ink and the mission

    A deforming nucleus and tapered brushstroke ribbons give the ink its own motion. Mission actions move the character and camera together, apply a visible city change and return control to the orbit view.

  3. 03

    Make the whole frame feel printed

    A post-processing pass adds restrained grain, edge darkening and halftone shadows. Color collection builds toward a final repainting of the district.

03 / the skills in the work

what this helped me practice.

The work develops procedural scene construction, shader design and interactive camera direction. It also explores how movement, sound and color changes can communicate progress without a dense interface.

  • Three.js
  • Procedural environments
  • GLSL shaders
  • Camera choreography
  • Interactive storytelling

a note on the process.

The clearest version gives one visual change to each action. Keeping the camera’s approach, contact and return in one sequence makes the heist readable as an interaction.
