NOX

A compact arena built around the rhythm of dashing, striking, and getting out of the way.

the curiositythe buildthe practice

01 / the question behind it

How much impact can come from a small arena and a few carefully timed actions?

NOX is a single-file Canvas arena game built around dashing, melee strikes and energy-powered bolts. Procedural drawing, particles, camera feedback and Web Audio synthesis give those actions a shared visual and sound identity.

02 / from idea to something playable

how the pieces came together.

  1. 01

    Separate the actions in time

    Dash speed and duration, melee wind-up and active frames, bolt cost and cooldowns are explicit configuration values. Their differences create the arena’s basic rhythm.

  2. 02

    Make a hit feel like an event

    Impact sound, glowing particles, rings and camera trauma respond to the action. The camera follows with easing and a slight lead toward the player’s aim.

  3. 03

    Keep the loop compact

    Movement, enemy spawning, energy rewards and the play/pause states live in a small browser build. The restricted scope keeps attention on the moment-to-moment fight.

03 / the skills in the work

what this helped me practice.

The project develops action timing, input handling and lightweight 2D rendering. It is a focused exercise in making a compact move set feel distinct through anticipation, impact and recovery.

  • Canvas 2D
  • JavaScript
  • Combat timing
  • Procedural effects
  • Web Audio

a note on the process.

A short pause, a clear flash and a distinct sound can make the same amount of damage feel very different. Consistent timing matters more than adding more effects.
